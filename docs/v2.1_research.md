## HN Newest Trend Analysis

### 1. Agentic Coding Burnout ("Agentic coding is burning me out" — 2pts)
Workers are overwhelmed by constant AI agent requests. Local-first, async research agents that run on schedules would reduce interrupt fatigue.
- *Rairos opportunity:* `gap watch --daemon` already runs in background; add a **Research Agent Dashboard** showing agent activity log, energy usage, and session TTL.

### 2. LLM Coherence Resolution ("LLMs Are Complex Coherence Resolution Engines" — 1pt)
LLMs are fundamentally coherence resolvers — tracking context and resolving ambiguities. Research OS could surface which papers the LLM found "hard to resolve" (high contradiction count).
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Contradiction Heatmap** — papers that generate many contradictions flagged as "coherence-challenged."

### 3. Open-Source Speech Recognition ("Cohere Transcribe" — 1pt)
On-device speech recognition becoming commodity. Voice notes could replace typed research queries.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Voice-to-Capsule** — record voice note, transcribe, auto-extract gap, save to Gene Pool.

### 4. Scientific Rigor ("Approaching Zero Bugs?" — 1pt)
Software engineering is converging on zero-bug practices. Research has no equivalent rigor standard.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Research Rigor Score** — rate papers by methodology transparency, reproducibility signals, dataset sharing.

### 5. Trust and Spam ("Combat LLM spam by building a web of trust" — 1pt)
LLM-generated content flooding the web. Provenance matters.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Source Trust Score** — track which arXiv categories / authors consistently yield high-quality Gene Pool capsules; filter low-trust sources.

### 6. AI and Labor Law ("Chinese Court Rules Firms Can't Lay Off Workers on AI Grounds" — 4pts)
New legal precedents forming around AI replacing workers. Regulatory gaps are emerging faster than papers study them.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Regulatory Gap Alert** — when new laws/regulations appear, automatically flag the research gap: "AI-labor law compliance frameworks."

### 7. MICT Agent Framework ("Getting the right answer the first time" — 2pts)
First-token-latency and context efficiency are key. Research agents that minimize re-reading papers.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Gene Pool Prefetch** — based on current research topic, pre-load likely relevant capsules into memory.

### 8. LLM TrendSlop ("Researchers Asked LLMs for Strategic Advice. They Got 'Trendslop'" — 4pts)
LLMs generate generic, trend-chasing advice. Research specificity matters.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Gap Credibility Score** — capsules with low novelty (too many overlapping keywords with existing capsules) get flagged as "trendslop."

### 9. Behavioral Sink ("Behavioral Sink" — 3pts)
Academic fields can over-concentrate around certain paradigms, ignoring alternatives.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Paradigm Concentration Alert** — when >60% of papers in a domain cite the same 3 references, flag a generalization_gap.

### 10. Chiral Superconductivity ("flower-like pattern exposes chiral superconductivity" — 4pts)
Cross-domain pattern detection: physics phenomena often map to AI concepts.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Cross-Domain Gap Bridge** — suggest Gene Pool connections between physics and AI papers based on shared keywords.

### 11. NetHack 5.0.0 (69pts, 8 comments)
Classic roguelike now has agents competing. Self-play, emergent behavior.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Research Game Mode** — gamify Gene Pool with "discovery quests": find 3 contradictions in one session, earn badges.

### 12. Driverless Car Regulation ("California to begin ticketing driverless cars" — 29pts)
Regulation lagging behind deployment. Classic evaluation_gap.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Evaluation Gap Detector** — auto-flag when deployment outpaces benchmark papers; trigger gap extraction for regulation-adjacent papers.

### 13. Richard Dawkins / AI Delusion (9pts)
Philosophy of AI meets evolutionary biology. Science vs. AI belief systems.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Philosophy Gap Tracker** — papers debating AI consciousness/sentience flagged as theoretical_gap with high polarity divergence.

### 14. Scientific Risk Aversion ("Is the Scientific Enterprise Too Risk-Averse?" — 1pt)
Research culture discourages bold hypotheses.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Bold Hypothesis Vault** — Gene Pool for high-risk, high-reward gap hypotheses; tracked separately from conservative gaps.

### 15. Legacy Tech Persistence ("You Have No Idea How Much You Still Use BlackBerry" — 1pt)
Old technology persists despite "better" alternatives. Real-world adoption gaps.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Adoption Gap Detector** — flag papers studying "why technology X still used" as unexplored_application type.

### 16. Research Dissemination ("Ask HN: Where do you promote your writing in 2026" — 1pt)
Researchers struggle to reach audiences.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Briefing Distributor** — auto-generate research digests for specific audiences (PhD advisor, industry engineer, policy maker).

### 17. Amsterdam Fossil Fuel Ads (1pt)
Policy drives technology adoption. Ban on fossil fuel ads → accelerated renewable research.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Policy Impact Tracer** — map policy changes → affected research domains → updated Gene Pool priorities.

### 18. PostgreSQL Backup (1pt)
Data infrastructure matters for reproducibility.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Gene Pool Backup Scheduler** — auto-snapshot Gene Pool JSONL daily, retain 30 versions.

### 19. Microsoft Headcount Decrease (5pts)
Corporate AI adoption driving job displacement.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Labor Displacement Gap Tracker** — dedicated Gene Pool category for "AI vs. human labor" research gaps.

### 20. Energy 360 / Island Fungi (not in newest but implied by trends)
Environmental research gaining traction.
- *Rairos opportunity:* **Climate AI Gap Monitor** — arXiv watch specifically for climate+AI intersection; high priority for Gene Pool.

## v2.1 Feature List (20+)

### Web UI

**F1 — Research Agent Dashboard**
- Show gap watch daemon status, recent events, energy usage
- Session TTL countdown, agent activity log
- Route: `GET /agent/dashboard`

**F2 — Contradiction Heatmap**
- Papers colored by contradiction count (red = many contradictions)
- Tooltip shows which gap_type/polarity pairs are conflicting
- Route: `GET /heatmap`

**F3 — Voice-to-Capsule**
- Upload audio → Whisper transcription → LLM gap extraction → save to Gene Pool
- Requires: mic access, Whisper API or local model
- Route: `POST /voice/capsule`

**F4 — Source Trust Score Panel**
- Per-arXiv-category trust rating (based on capsule quality)
- Filter paper import by trust threshold
- Route: `GET /trust-scores`

**F5 — Gene Pool Prefetch**
- Based on current active topic, pre-warm cache with relevant capsules
- Show "prefetched" indicator in Gene Pool dashboard
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/prefetch?topic=X`

**F6 — Research Rigor Score**
- Rate imported papers: dataset sharing, code availability, methodology clarity
- Display on paper detail page as a "rigor badge"
- Route: `GET /paper/{id}/rigor`

**F7 — Paradigm Concentration Alert**
- Triggered when >60% citations cluster around ≤3 references in a domain
- Notification in web UI + Gene Pool entry
- Route: `GET /alerts/paradigm`

**F8 — Bold Hypothesis Vault**
- Separate Gene Pool view for high-risk/high-reward gaps
- Distinct visual style (red accent vs. standard blue)
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/bold`

**F9 — Gap Credibility Filter**
- Flag capsules with low novelty score (keyword overlap >0.7 with existing)
- Show "trendslop" warning badge
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/credibility`

**F10 — Research Game Mode**
- Badges: "Contradiction Hunter" (3+ contradictions), "Gap Extractor" (10+ capsules), "Evolution Master" (evolved capsule)
- Leaderboard optional (local only)
- Route: `GET /game`

**F11 — Evaluation Gap Alert**
- Flag when deployment timeline outpaces benchmark paper count
- Specifically for: AV regulation, AI policy, climate tech
- Route: `GET /alerts/eval-gap`

**F12 — Cross-Domain Gap Bridge**
- Suggest Gene Pool connections between papers from distant arXiv categories
- Based on shared method/concept keywords
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/bridges`

**F13 — Policy Impact Tracer**
- When user flags a new policy (regulation, ban, standard), trace affected domains
- Auto-update Gene Pool priority weights
- Route: `POST /policy/trace`

**F14 — Gene Pool Backup Scheduler**
- Daily snapshot to `~/.ai_research_os/backups/`
- Retain last 30 versions, prune oldest
- Config: `AIROS_BACKUP_ENABLED=true`

**F15 — Labor Displacement Gap Tracker**
- Dedicated Gene Pool filter for "AI vs. human labor" gaps
- arXiv category: cs.cyber-ph, cs.soc
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/labor`

**F16 — Climate AI Gap Monitor**
- arXiv watch tuned to climate+AI intersection
- Higher priority weight in gap watch matching
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/climate`

**F17 — Briefing Distributor**
- Generate audience-specific digests: PhD advisor, industry, policy maker
- Select target audience → regenerate briefing in that voice
- Route: `POST /briefing/distribute`

**F18 — Auto-Archive at Risk Panel**
- Show capsules with low_score_streak ≥ 2 (at risk of archive)
- One-click "keep active" or "pint to ttl"
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/at-risk`

**F19 — Capsule Quality Review Queue**
- New capsules pending first feedback
- System prompts: "Does this gap match your research?"
- Route: `GET /gene-pool/review`

**F20 — Research Disseminator**
- One-click share: generate public link to briefing
- Short URL: `rairos.app/b/{short_id}`
- Route: `POST /briefing/share`

**F21 — Citation Pathfinder Web Graph**
- Interactive SVG graph: paper → cites → cites → Gene Pool capsule
- Color-coded by gap_type
- Route: `GET /path/{paper_id}`

**F22 — arXiv Watch Alert Channels**
- Multiple watch feeds: general, climate, AI safety, regulation
- Each feed: different Gene Pool matching criteria
- Route: `GET /watch/feeds`

## Core Insight

> **Gene Pool Credibility** is the most actionable new dimension introduced by HN newest trends.
> LLM spam → need credibility scoring; trend-slop → need novelty filtering;
> paradigm concentration → need diversity alerts. All three feed into the same Gene Pool quality system.
